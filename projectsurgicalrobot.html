Surgical Robot System

A design project toward a da Vinci-style surgical robot, starting with the problem of arms colliding during surgery.

TimelineSept 2026 – present
ToolsSolidWorks
FocusArm collisions & workspace

Overview

I want to build surgical technology like the da Vinci system, so I started by studying how existing robots such as da Vinci and Maestro are built and how their arms sit around a patient.

The problem I'm tackling

When several arms work close together, they can collide, which limits workspace and constrains port placement. My approach is a thinner, sleeker arm that leaves more clearance between arms.

Progress

  • Researched the da Vinci and Maestro systems
  • Began modeling initial cable-driven arm components in SolidWorks
  • Building toward a complete, working CAD model of the arm
