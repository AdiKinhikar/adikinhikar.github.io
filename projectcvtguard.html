CVT Guard

A redesigned CVT guard for Olin's Baja SAE vehicle, built around the rulebook and made to come off quickly.

TeamOlin Baja SAE, Drivetrain
ToolsOnshape
RulesBSAE 2027 Rev A

Overview

The new guard replaces a heavy, bulky old one. I worked on the design with one other teammate, and we presented it at a design review.

Rules first

Before designing, I gathered every CVT guarding rule from the BSAE 2027 Rev A rulebook. I also checked our firewall mounting method against the 120+ page rulebook.

Design

  • Sheet metal that follows the pulley shape, with a shorter main band
  • Front and back plate cutouts that double as ventilation ports
  • A color-coded fastener system for part identification and faster pit stops

Easy removal

Four McMaster-Carr Style K quick-release latches, two per side, sit halfway along the guard. The guard splits into two pieces, which gives easier and greater reach to the pulley when it's off.

Next steps

  • Structural tests for the guard and its mounts
  • Size and weight numbers
  • Finalize vent geometry and cost
  • Add latch mounting holes
