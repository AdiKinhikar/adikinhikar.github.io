5-DOF Robotic Arm

A 3D printed robotic arm designed from the ground up so I understand every mechanism in it.

Degrees of freedom5
CADOnshape
ControllerArduino Uno R3

Why I'm building it

The goal is learning. Instead of buying off-the-shelf mechanisms, I'm designing the joints and gearboxes myself.

Hardware

  • Five NEMA 17 stepper motors (2 A, 55 N·cm rated) with TMC2209 drivers
  • Arduino Uno R3 as the controller
  • SG90 micro servo for the gripper
  • Upper arm 9 in, forearm 7 in, with parts CAD'd in Onshape and 3D printed

Motion and torque

Arm motion is planned with inverse kinematics. Each stepper drives its joint through a two-stage, roughly 10:1 planetary gearbox, which turns the motor's 55 N·cm rating into about 5.5 N·m of theoretical output torque before efficiency losses.

Electronics

I practiced the Arduino, TMC2209, and NEMA 17 wiring in Cirkit Designer before building the real circuit.
