Braille Reader

Helping build a braille reader that makes written text more accessible to blind users.

TeamOlin Assistive Technology Lab
SubteamMechanical & Actuations
FocusElectromagnetic cam

Overview

The braille reader aims to give blind users better access to written text. I'm on the Mechanical & Actuations subteam, working on the electromagnetic cams.

The challenge

Each cam's solenoid has to be sized correctly, meaning its length, thickness, and number of turns. I began by investigating copper wire coils to inform the design.

My calculation plan

  1. Find the coil's minimum and maximum magnetic force, based on the braille reader's magnet and the attached mass
  2. Solve for the number of turns and current using the electromagnet force equation, with an optimization script
  3. Choose the wire gauge from an AWG chart

The plan is written, and the calculations are next.
