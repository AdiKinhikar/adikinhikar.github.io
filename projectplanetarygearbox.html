Two-Stage Planetary Gearbox

A compact two-stage planetary gearbox, about 10:1 overall, built around a 2 in ring gear.

ReductionAbout 10:1
Ring gear2 in
Gear typeSingle helical

Why a planetary gearbox

The arm's NEMA 17 motors are fast but low in torque. A planetary gearbox trades speed for torque in a small, coaxial package that fits inside an arm joint.

Design

  • Stage 1: 24-tooth sun gear with three 12-tooth planet gears
  • Stage 2: 20-tooth sun gear with 14-tooth planet gears
  • 2 in ring gear, with all dimensions in inches

Gear choice

I'm using single helical gears. They run smoother than straight-cut gears, and they're far simpler to design and make than double helical (herringbone) gears.
